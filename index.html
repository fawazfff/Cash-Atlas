Questions

The useful details.

What is Second Life?

A tool that suggests useful next lives for objects before they are thrown away.

Does it actually recycle the item?

No. It helps you decide whether to repair, reuse, donate, transform, or correctly recycle it.

Is this a finished commercial product?

Not yet. This is a working hackathon prototype demonstrating the core experience.

What are you entering it for?

Frontier Cascadia's AI for Good track and Frontier Award.
